### Project Areas:
1. Applied population genetics (e.g. inference of population structure, genetic diversity and natural selection)
2. Applied disease genetics (e.g. genome-wide association studies)
3. Methods development within both the above topics (e.g. for inference of relatedness)

If you are interested in doing a MSc thesis or Bioinformatics project, send an [email to Ida](mailto:ida@bio.ku.dk) to arrange a meeting for discussing research interests.
