### Project Areas:
1. Model consequences of genomic protein variants that lead to disease
2. Model effects of posttranslational modifications (such as phosphorylations) on protein structure and functions
3. Develop protocols to identify novel protein interaction interfaces
4. Language models for protein-protein interactions

If you are interested in doing a MSc thesis or Bioinformatics project, send an [email to Amelie](mailto:amelie.stein@bio.ku.dk) to arrange a meeting for discussing research interests.
